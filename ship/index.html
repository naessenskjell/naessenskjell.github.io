Welcome aboard, traveler!

Hey there and welcome to my little corner of the galaxy! This ship is very much under construction, so expect things to change, break and move around. The page is not very mobile friendly at this moment, but I hope to fix that in the future. In the meantime, please enjoy the view from your desktop or laptop.

Every page is a place to explore. Pick out your destination in the viewfinder to set a course by simply clicking on one of the objects. Or use the menu on the left to navigate using the LCARS interface. Many of the planets have moons and satellites orbiting them, which you can often visit on an away-mission. Have fun exploring, and leave a note in the subspace guestbook if you want.
