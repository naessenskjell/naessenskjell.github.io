Observation records

“The difference between screwing around and science is writing it down.”
– Adam Savage

When visiting a new country, the first thing to do is find any nature to observe and record. You could say it is because of my fascination with nature, or because I like to collect data, but I think it is a bit of both. I have been recording most of my nature observations for some years in observation.org, especially the ones that are more interesting or unusual. Below you will find some of the totals, and some beautiful graphics. The totals are kept in a database in orbit, but is updated automatically when antennas align.
